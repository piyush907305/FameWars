2. Eligibility: approved age/India launch scope, honest eligibility information; under-18 policy resolved before access. No gambling, stakes or cash rewards; cosmetic items have no cash redemption or transfer.
3. Fair play: no cheating, flooding, security abuse, hateful/community/religion targeting, harassment, election messaging or infringement. No player uploads/chat in initial scope. Legitimate bug reports via private contact, not exploitation.
4. Accounts later: guest limitations, optional provider login, secure account handling, deletion and suspension/appeal process. No assurance that client-local cosmetic records survive reset; explain before opt-in.
5. Ads later: clear optional reward terms and provider policies, no ads during combat, no guarantee of ad availability/revenue/reward without verified completion. Privacy choices separate from terms acceptance; do not force blanket optional-processing consent.
6. Intellectual property: game/operator rights subject to third-party licences; public attribution and content licence notices. No permission to extract/resell other providers' asset libraries. Generated asset exclusivity/copyright not promised.
7. Content reports: rights/privacy contact, fighter/build ID, review/takedown and appeal process; availability may change under remote disable. No public allegations or personal data in report descriptions.
8. Availability: no uninterrupted-network promise; maintenance, disconnect, manifest expiry and offline limits described. Refund/payment terms only if future payments introduced after separate review; no payments now.
9. Privacy: link actual approved bilingual notice; identify purposes and user rights, not blanket consent buried in terms.
10. Legal/dispute provisions: local counsel drafts applicable consumer protections, liability/disclaimers, governing jurisdiction and grievance escalation. Avoid waiving statutory rights or inventing compulsory arbitration.
11. Changes/contact: versioning, notice of material changes and real monitored operator route. Future native/global/ranking/UGC scope reviewed before release.

Owner must approve final English and Hindi terms and privacy copy before public launch. Introductory disclaimer cannot replace these policies or licensing clearance.
