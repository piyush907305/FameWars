## Proposed plain-language notice structure

**Who we are and who can play.** Fame Wars is an original cartoon fighting game for the approved launch age group in India. Proposed age is 18+ (A01); not yet finalized. We use an age-band choice without storing full date of birth; this is not identity verification.

**What the local prototype uses.** Your controls, language, sound settings and optional locally stored preferences. There are no player recordings, voice uploads, celebrity imitation, accounts, online opponents, advertising or tracking SDKs in v0.1.0. A hosting provider may process network/security logs; list the chosen provider and actual retention before external hosting. “Local game” is not a claim that host logs never exist.

**Optional later purposes.** With appropriate choice/eligibility, online rooms need a guest/account identifier and match inputs; analytics measures reliability and engagement; ads use selected provider data and consent controls. Describe each purpose, data category, actual recipient and retention separately before enabling. No precise location, contacts, biometric inputs or microphone collection planned. Synthetic voices are pre-generated asset files, not recordings of players.

**Your choices.** Essential operation and optional analytics/ads separated. Decline optional purposes; change or withdraw choices in Settings → Privacy. Explain any essential service consequences without coercion. No covert tracking/fingerprinting after refusal. Clear local preferences on request; later server deletion and backup schedule explained accurately.

**Sharing and service providers.** Name approved hosting/auth/ads/analytics providers, their purposes, countries and policy links; distinguish processors/independent provider roles after legal review. No blanket “we never share data” promise when vendors process it. No data sale plan.

**Retention and security.** Publish actual approved schedule from [policy plan](09-legal-and-content-policy.md), not unapproved defaults. Describe TLS/access controls proportionately, no perfect-security claim. Explain legal holds/required logs and incident contact.

**Requests and complaints.** Contact named operator for access/correction/erasure/grievance/nomination where applicable. Explain verification, response route and applicable escalation to authority. Do not invent statutory response time; map current law before publishing. Child-related request workflow must match actual audience design.

**Updates.** Version notices, show changes of purposes before use and obtain renewed choice when required. Maintain bilingual version parity. International expansion/native ads require updated notice before launch.
